Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to common questions.

Is MsgVault safe and private?

Yes, 100%. MsgVault does NOT have any external servers to collect your messages. Everything is stored locally on your own phone inside an AES-256 SQLCipher encrypted database.

Does MsgVault drain my battery?

No. MsgVault uses Android's native NotificationListenerService which is triggered only when a new message notification arrives. It consumes 0% battery while idle.

What if my phone says "Restricted Setting"?

On Android 13/14+, tap the button inside MsgVault ➔ Open App Info ➔ Tap 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner ➔ Tap "Allow restricted settings". Then enable Notification Access.
