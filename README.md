# 🛡️ MsgVault – Secure Offline Notification & Media Saver
**Developed by Dhiraj**

MsgVault is a privacy-first, 100% offline notification saver and deleted media recovery utility for Android. All your messages, media, and notification logs are stored inside an encrypted SQLCipher database right on your device.

## ✨ Key Features
1. **Full Notification Vault**: Automatically archives incoming notifications from WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, SMS, and any other apps you choose.
2. **WhatsApp Media Recovery**: Captures and saves photos and media attachments even if deleted by sender.
3. **Direct WhatsApp Chat**: Start WhatsApp conversations instantly without saving unknown phone numbers to your contacts.
4. **100% Offline SQLCipher Encryption**: Your database is encrypted using 256-bit AES with keys safeguarded inside the hardware Android Keystore.
5. **Zero Cloud Guarantee**: MsgVault has zero internet servers for user messages. Your personal chats never leave your physical phone.

---

## 📱 How to Install & Configure
1. Download and install MsgVault.apk.
2. Open the app and grant **Notification Access** permissions.
3. *If your phone shows "Restricted Setting" (Android 13/14+)*:
   - Tap **"🔓 Phone says 'Restricted Setting'? Tap Here"**.
   - Tap **"Open App Info ➔"**.
   - In App Info, tap the three dots (**⋮**) in the top right corner and tap **"Allow restricted settings"**.
   - Return to MsgVault and enable Notification Access.
4. Choose the apps you want MsgVault to monitor.
